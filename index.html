MEET THE CHEF

Chef Luigi is from the small town of Bellagio Italy. From the young age of 7 Luigi showed his interest in cooking and in his heritage. Starting out in his grandmother’s kitchen helping her prepare for family dinners he later went on to graduate from the Florence Culinary Art School. After he came to the US and moved to Raleigh to become the Executive Chef at Margaux’s. He was the Executive Chef there for 6 years before leaving to open his own restaurant where he hopes to go back to his roots and give each person who enters a taste of his grandmother’s food.
